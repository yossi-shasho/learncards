**Introduction:**

The **Impossible Location** is a mind-blowing card trick that combines elements of **Mental Tricks**, **Memory Tricks**, and **Intermediate Tricks**. With this trick, you’ll reveal a card that the spectator has merely thought of, without even touching the deck! This routine requires a mix of memory, misdirection, and audience engagement, making it perfect for magicians who want to elevate their performance to the next level.

This trick works by utilizing a secret memorization technique to track the location of cards in the deck, creating the illusion of an impossible mental connection between the magician and the spectator.

**Performance:**

**Materials:**

• A regular deck of cards.

**Setup:**

1\. **Shuffling the Deck**: Begin by shuffling the deck thoroughly in front of the audience. Let the spectator cut the deck to ensure they believe everything is random. This enhances the illusion of fairness and eliminates any suspicion of setup.

2\. **Memorizing the Order**: As you shuffle, memorize the top five cards in the deck. You don’t need to memorize the entire deck—just focus on those first five cards. Let’s assume the top five cards are **King of Diamonds**, **Ten of Spades**, **Four of Clubs**, **Ace of Hearts**, and **Nine of Diamonds**.

**Execution:**

3\. **Spectator Thinks of a Card**: Spread the deck face up on the table and ask the spectator to merely **think** of any card they see without touching it or telling you what it is. The key here is that, by spreading the cards face up, you’ve already memorized the top five cards, which are now spread among the rest of the deck.

4\. **The Secret Memorization**: As the spectator looks at the cards, secretly focus on your five memorized cards and their positions. If they seem to focus on one of these cards (let’s say they’re thinking of the **Ace of Hearts**), you’ll know exactly where it is in the deck when you collect the cards.

5\. **Misdirection and Shuffle**: Once the spectator has mentally selected their card, gather the cards back up and give the deck a false shuffle, making it appear random but keeping track of the key cards’ locations.

**The Impossible Location:**

6\. **The Revelation Build-Up**: Now, ask the spectator to concentrate on their thought-of card while you pretend to “tune in” to their mind. Add some theatrics here, such as closing your eyes or lightly running your fingers over the deck, claiming to sense their card.

7\. **Locating the Card**: With the knowledge of where the **Ace of Hearts** is in the deck, confidently cut the deck right at the location of that card, or start dealing cards from the top until you reach it.

**The Big Reveal:**

8\. **The Impossible Reveal**: Dramatically reveal the **Ace of Hearts**, presenting it as the card they were merely thinking of. The audience will be stunned because they never physically chose or touched the card, yet you were able to locate it with precision.

9\. **Extra Flourish (Optional)**: To add even more astonishment, after revealing the card, you can perform a final flourish, such as asking the spectator to guess which position their card was in the deck, further playing into the impossibility of the moment.

**Conclusion:**

The **Impossible Location** is a powerful mentalism trick that uses memorization and a bit of showmanship to create an unforgettable moment for your audience. By mastering the memory aspect and the subtlety of misdirection, you’ll deliver a performance that leaves your spectators completely baffled. This trick is perfect for magicians looking to step up their mentalism game and leave a lasting impression.
