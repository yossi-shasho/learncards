**Effect:** The magician shuffles a normal deck of cards, he shows it to the audience, he starts at the bottom and finds the first three cards with the same number, lets say 6…

(so 6C, 6S, 6H), he puts these three cards on the table. The magician then takes a card from the top of the deck (2 of spades), and lays it on the table beside the three 6’s. He then takes a 6 (6H) and places it on top of the 2S. The magician repeats this step 2 more times with the 6C and 6S, giving him a small stack cards with alternating sixes.

The magician puts this small stack of cards on top the deck. He then removes 3 cards back off the top and they are all 6 cards (6H, 6S, DC)! The magician ask a spectator to blow the card on top of the deck, and when he shows it to the spectator it is the other 6 (6D)!!

#### Trick Explanation:

1\. After shuffling the deck you fan the deck to proof that its not a tricked deck, but you have to look to the third card, lets say its the 6D, then you tell the spectator that from the bottom you will take off the first three 6 cards you see (6H, 6S, 6C).

2\. You put a card on the table from the top of the deck, then you put a 6, another card, then another 6 and another card from top of deck that you know is a 6, then you put another six.

3\. you put them back on top of the deck, then you pick the three first cards and put it face down on the table, you tell the spectator to look at them.

4\. then you ask the spectator to blow the first card on the deck and you throw it on table.
