Hello! Thanks for stopping by.

Learncards started in 2007 for a simple reason: I love card tricks, and I kept finding that the explanations online were either buried in long videos or written for people who already knew the jargon. I wanted a plain page per trick, with the steps written out in order, that you could prop up next to a deck and follow along with.

That's still all this site is. Every trick has its own page. Most start with the **effect**, which is what your audience sees, and then walk through the **method**, which is what you actually do. If a write-up uses a word you don't know, the [glossary](/glossary/) should sort you out.

## Who it's for

Anyone with a deck of cards. Kids learning their first trick, grown-ups who want something to show at the next family dinner, and hobbyists like me who just enjoy the puzzle of how a trick works. You don't need to buy anything. A regular, slightly battered deck is perfect.

## How the tricks are chosen

I look for tricks that work with an ordinary deck and fool real people. Some are old classics, some were sent in by readers over the years, and a few are variations on each other, because seeing two versions of the same idea is a great way to understand it. Each one is sorted by difficulty and by type, so you can start with the [easy tricks](/category/easy-tricks/) or the [self-working tricks](/category/self-working-tricks/) and work your way up.

## What you won't find here

No ads, no pop-ups, no tracking and no JavaScript. The pages load fast and work on any old computer or phone, which feels right for a site about something as low-tech as a deck of cards.

## One last thing

Practice! Read a trick, try it slowly, then try it again. Do it for yourself a few times before you do it for anyone else. The first time somebody gasps at something you did with your own hands is a wonderful feeling, and I hope this site helps you get there.
